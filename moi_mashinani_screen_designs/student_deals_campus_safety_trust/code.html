The 5-Point Campus Safety & Trust Pact

Off-campus commerce can sometimes expose first years and continuing students to fake deposits, bait-and-switch electronics repairs, and unresponsive fundis. Here is how MoiMashinani shields you:

01 storefront

Physical Kiosk Verification

We physically walk every alley in Kesses Centre, Stage, Soweto, and Cheboiywo. If a vendor does not possess a permanent physical bench, room, or kiosk, they are strictly prohibited from listing.

02 account_balance_wallet

Safaricom Till Plate Match

Student ambassadors inspect the official green Lipa Na M-Pesa till plate at the shop counter against business registry records to eliminate fraudulent personal send-money numbers.

03 do_not_disturb_on

Zero Upfront Repair Deposit Rule

Never send 100% advance deposit for phone or laptop parts. Approved tech fundis only receive partial deposits after signing a physical triage ticket in person inside their registered shop.

04 price_change

Pinned Transparent Student Pricing

No inflated pricing when merchants spot freshers. Every verified partner agrees to display a visible, pinned student price rate card directly on their stall wall.

05 report

2-Hour Rapid De-Listing Rule

Any registered vendor who refuses an advertised discount, switches off their line, or defrauds a comrade is temporarily locked out within 120 minutes of ambassador investigation.
