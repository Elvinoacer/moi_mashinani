store About Kevin’s Workshop (Kesses Centre)

Operating continuously for over 4 years right behind the Equity Bank ATM at Kesses Centre, Kevin Phones & Laptops has become the go-to technical workstation for Moi University engineering, IT, and computer science students. Rather than relying on unreliable grey-market boards, Kevin personally receives twice-weekly consignments of genuine original screens, OEM laptop panels, and high-purity solder paste directly sourced from vetted importers on Nairobi’s Luthuli Avenue.

Every replacement is conducted in open view on an anti-static workbench, guaranteeing complete anti-counterfeit integrity and peace of mind for sensitive student coursework and hardware. If a device has sustained water damage or drop-impact fractures, students receive an honest assessment and a transparent diagnostic report before any repair work commences.
